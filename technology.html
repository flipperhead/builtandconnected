The Technology Checkup

The Checkup is a visit of about two hours where I look over the technology in your home. It catches small problems early, like a router still using the password it came with.

Afterward, you get a written report to keep. It says what I found, what to fix first, and what can wait. If you'd like me to make the fixes, you'll get a written estimate first.

$295 Flat fee for homes

I quote businesses and rental properties by size, usually after a short phone call. The call is free.

What I look at

  • Your devices: I list what's connected to your network and flag anything that shouldn't be there.
  • Backups: I check that you have one, then restore a file from it so you know it works.
  • Accounts: I look at your email, banking, and cloud sign-ins, and how you'd get back in if you were locked out.
  • Router and Wi-Fi: I check the router's password, its built-in software, the guest network, and whether the signal reaches every room.
  • Updates: I note what's out of date and anything that no longer gets updates at all.
  • Virus protection: I check what's installed, whether it's working, and whether you're paying for more than one.
  • A written report: It's in plain language, with the most important fixes listed first.

When something's wrong

The computer is slow, stuck, or acting strange

I work on Windows and Mac computers. I find out what's wrong first, then we decide together whether to fix it or replace it.

Wi-Fi that drops, crawls, or won't reach

I fix dead spots, like a back bedroom or a deck the signal won't reach. I also track down a connection that drops at the same time every evening.

Email that won't send or sync

I fix messages that won't send and mailboxes that stopped updating. I also help you get back into an account you've been locked out of.

Printers that won't print or connect

I get printers working again, whether the problem is the printer, the computer, or the Wi-Fi.

Something looks like a scam

If a text, email, pop-up, or phone call seems wrong, call me before you click a link or call anyone back.

Lost photos and files

I try to recover photos and documents from computers and drives that stopped working. Before I start, I'll tell you how likely it is to work.

Setting things up

New computer setup and moving your files

I move your files and settings to the new computer and set it up the way you work. Then I erase the old one before it leaves the house.

Wi-Fi for your home or office

I place the router where it reaches the rooms you use, and add a second Wi-Fi unit if needed. I also set up a separate network for guests.

Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace

I set up email, calendars, and file storage so they work on all your devices. Your files stay in one place instead of three.

Network cable, mounting, and equipment cabinets

I run network cable through walls, mount cameras and Wi-Fi units, and build cabinets to hold the equipment. This is where my carpentry comes in handy.

Smart locks and thermostats for rentals

I set them up with the accounts in your name, so you stay in control. More for rental owners →

Keeping your accounts and files safe

Backups you've seen work

I set up a backup for your computer. Then I restore a file in front of you so you know the backup works.

Secure your email and other accounts

I turn on two-step sign-in for the accounts that matter, so a stolen password isn't enough to get in. I also update your recovery details and set up a password manager.

Learning to spot scams

We spend about an hour going through real scam texts, emails, and calls, one-on-one or with a small group. You keep a one-page guide by the phone.

Tightening your router's settings

I update the router's built-in software and replace the password it came with. I turn off settings that let someone reach it from outside, and keep guests off your own network.

For small businesses

Computer and network help for small businesses

I work with small offices, lodges, shops, and restaurants. These businesses depend on their computers but don't need an IT company on a monthly contract.

The work is the same as for homes: the network, the accounts, the backups, and the computers. For a business, an outage can cost a day of sales, so I fix what keeps you open first.

How it works

  • Start with a Checkup: I quote it by the size of the business, so we both know what we're working with.
  • Get a written estimate: You get a detailed estimate before any work starts. If anything changes, I tell you right away.
  • Fix what matters first: We work through the report in order of importance, not all at once.
  • Call when you need me: There's no contract, no minimum, and no monthly fee.
  • Come back once or twice a year: You can book a follow-up visit to keep things up to date.

What I don't offer

  • Monthly IT contracts or overnight monitoring
  • 24-hour emergency service
  • Alarm systems, satellite dishes, or TV repair
  • Electrical work

If you need one of these, I'll point you to someone who does.

Most jobs start with a short call about what's happening. Sometimes I can fix it over the phone in ten minutes. That first call is free.
