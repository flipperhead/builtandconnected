Technology

Making home and small-business technology work — and keep working

Most technology trouble isn't dramatic. It's a backup nobody ever tested, a router still using its factory password, an email account with no second layer of protection. I find those things and fix them, then explain what I did in plain language.

  • Technology checkups, with a written report you keep
  • Network and Wi-Fi setup, cleanup, and hardening
  • Backups that get tested, not just switched on
  • Passwords, accounts, and second-factor sign-in
  • Learning to spot scams, phishing, and fake calls
  • New computer setup and moving your files over
  • Vacation rental technology — guest Wi-Fi, locks, thermostats

Carpentry

From framing to finish, decks to doors

I came up in the carpentry trade and the craftsmanship is the part I still enjoy most. Decks, remodels, and repairs are the bulk of it — work that often starts with tearing something out and ends with something that looks like it belongs. Weather up here is hard on outdoor woodwork, and I build with that in mind.

  • Decks — new builds, rebuilds, railings, and repairs
  • Remodels and renovations, room by room
  • Doors, windows, and framing
  • Trim, molding, and finish carpentry
  • Built-ins, shelving, and storage
  • Rot repair, structural fixes, and weathered rebuilds

Sized to the job — a single door or a whole floor. If you're not sure whether it's something I do, ask. Photographs of past work are coming to this website soon.
